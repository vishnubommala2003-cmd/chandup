Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Viseshta Group ("we", "us", or "our"), founded in 2018 in Hyderabad, values the privacy of our website visitors, clients, and prospective property investors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information submitted across our digital platforms, including inquiries regarding Invesmart, Palm Valley, Teak Village, and Vistara Valley.

2. Information We Collect

When you request property breakdowns, brochures, or site visits through our enquiry forms, we collect:

  • Full name and contact details (telephone number, WhatsApp number, email address)
  • Investment preferences, desired unit sizing, and budget range
  • Preferred times of contact and communication channel preferences
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser cookies to improve platform functionality

3. Purpose and Use of Data

We use collected data solely to:

  • Transmit requested property breakdowns, pricing sheets, and floor plans via Email or WhatsApp
  • Coordinate verified site visits to our development locations in Greater Hyderabad
  • Provide statutory RERA compliance dockets and project progress updates
  • Respond directly to customer inquiries and service requests

4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We maintain a strict zero-spam, non-disclosure policy. Your personal information is never sold, leased, or traded to unauthorized third-party marketing brokers. Data is accessible solely to authorized Viseshta Group investment consultants.

5. Opt-Out and Contact

You may withdraw consent to receive communications at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or sending an email to privacy@viseshtagroup.com.
